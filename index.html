How it works

1

Drag the button to your bookmarks bar

Click and drag "🧹 Scrub Community" up to your browser's bookmarks bar. This saves the tool for reuse.

2

Go to your X Community

Navigate to x.com/i/communities/your-community — the one you want to clean.

3

Click the bookmark

A small control panel appears in the corner. Pick your speed and hit "Start Scrubbing". It deletes posts one by one with random, human-like timing.

4

Walk away

It runs in the background. Takes breaks automatically. Stops after a safe session limit. Come back to a clean community.

FAQ

Will this get my account banned?

The tool mimics natural human behavior with random delays and breaks. It's clicking the same buttons you would manually. That said, use at your own risk — we recommend the "Careful" speed setting for important accounts.

Does it delete MY posts or everyone's?

It clicks delete on posts visible in the community feed. As a community admin, you can delete any post. It processes them in order from top to bottom as you'd see them on the page.

Can I use it on mobile?

Bookmarklets don't work well on mobile browsers. This is a desktop tool — Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Brave.

How long does it take?

Depends on post count and speed. On "Normal" speed: ~100 posts/hour. A community with 1,000 posts takes ~10 hours across a few sessions. On "Fast": ~200/hour.
